MULTI-AGENT AI PIPELINE

How ReturnGuard Works

A chain of specialized AI agents work together to verify every return with visual evidence

01

📦 Delivery Evidence

Multi-angle photos are captured at delivery and background-removed. DINOv2 generates a product embedding as ground truth.

02

🧬 Embedding Comparison

When a return is initiated, DINOv2 embeddings are generated for the return images and compared using cosine similarity across all angles.

03

📐 Similarity Router

If similarity > 80%, the return is auto-accepted. Otherwise, images are routed to the Vision-Language Model for deep analysis.

04

👁️ VLM Deep Analysis

Three parallel VLM agents analyze front, back, and side views independently — comparing product shape, color, labels, and damage.

05

⚖️ Final Judge

A final LLM synthesizes all three VLM reviews into a unified verdict with evidence summary and confidence level.

06

📊 Decision Output

Every decision ships with a full evidence report. Accept, escalate to human review, or reject — all with explainable reasoning.
